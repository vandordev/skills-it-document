# Postmortem Reference

Use this reference when writing an incident review or failure analysis.

## Purpose

A postmortem explains what happened, why it happened, how it was fixed, and what should change to prevent recurrence.

## Recommended Structure

1. Summary
2. Impact
3. Timeline
4. Root Cause
5. Contributing Factors
6. Detection and Response
7. What Went Well
8. What Went Poorly
9. Corrective Actions
10. Follow-Up Owners

## Writing Rules

- Keep it blameless and factual.
- Separate timeline facts from interpretation.
- Make action items concrete and owned.
- Highlight systemic issues, not just the final failure.

## Quality Check

- A reader should understand both the failure and the prevention plan.
- The action items should be specific enough to track.
