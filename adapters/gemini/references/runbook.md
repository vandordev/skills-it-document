# Runbook Reference

Use this reference when documenting a repeatable operational procedure.

## Purpose

A runbook tells someone what to do when a known operational situation happens.

## Recommended Structure

1. Purpose
2. Triggers
3. Prerequisites
4. Detection / Symptoms
5. Diagnosis
6. Actions
7. Rollback / Recovery
8. Escalation
9. Verification
10. Post-Action Notes

## Writing Rules

- Make steps executable under pressure.
- Keep the order strict and unambiguous.
- Include commands, checks, and stop conditions when relevant.
- Prefer short steps over explanatory prose.

## Good Runbook Triggers

- incident response
- maintenance window
- support escalation
- routine operational task
- production rollback

## Quality Check

- A responder should be able to follow the document without prior tribal knowledge.
