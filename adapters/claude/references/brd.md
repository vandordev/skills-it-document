# BRD Reference

Use this reference when drafting a business requirements document.

## Purpose

A BRD explains the business case, market context, monetization, constraints, and success criteria behind the product.

## Recommended Structure

1. Ringkasan
2. Latar Belakang
3. Tujuan Bisnis
4. Target Market
5. Problem Statement
6. Value Proposition
7. Ruang Lingkup Produk
8. Model Bisnis
9. Metrik Bisnis
10. Risiko dan Mitigasi

## Writing Rules

- Focus on business value, not implementation detail.
- Make targets measurable whenever possible.
- State positioning clearly so the product has a sharp market fit.
- Use tables for segmentation, pricing, metrics, and risk.
- Keep the document decision-oriented. Avoid long history unless it changes the business case.

## Section Guidance

- Ringkasan: one-paragraph explanation of what the product is and who it serves.
- Latar Belakang: the market problem and why the product exists.
- Tujuan Bisnis: revenue, retention, activation, or validation goals.
- Target Market: primary and secondary segments, ideally with examples.
- Problem Statement: the specific business and customer pain being solved.
- Value Proposition: why this product should win for each target segment.
- Ruang Lingkup Produk: what is in MVP and what is explicitly out of scope.
- Model Bisnis: plans, pricing, limits, and any early-user or lifetime offers.
- Metrik Bisnis: North Star metric, primary metrics, and secondary signals.
- Risiko dan Mitigasi: business risks, adoption risks, and how to reduce them.

## Quality Check

- The market segment should be obvious.
- The pricing or monetization logic should be explicit.
- Metrics should reflect the product’s actual business motion.
- Risks should be tied to realistic adoption or retention failure modes.
